# Audio credits

Every sound here is public domain (CC0 1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/):
free for commercial use, no attribution required. We credit the makers anyway.

Each sound ships twice, as `.webm` (Opus) and `.m4a` (AAC), trimmed, faded out and peak-normalised
from the original. Volumes are set in `src/fx/sound.ts`.
